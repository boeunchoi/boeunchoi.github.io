My research centers on understanding how gas flows in and around galaxies and how it shapes the evolution of galaxies. Using the extensive data for the Milky Way, I track and characterize these flows and the physical processes that govern them. Most recently, I have been studying how star formation and gas flows regulate each other. I especially love working with spectral data and digging into how we reconstruct the physical conditions of gas from it.
