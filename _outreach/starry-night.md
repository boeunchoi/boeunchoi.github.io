During my undergraduate years at Sejong University, I participated in several outreach activities. The biggest activity was the [Starry Night Festival](https://pr.sejong.ac.kr/news/newsletter/engineering-news-eng.do?mode=view&articleNo=798092), a long-running outreach program of the Astronomy department, led mainly by undergraduate students. Over 2-3 days, it offers telescope observing, a mobile planetarium, lectures, hands-on crafts, and exhibitions for the public, from children and high school students to university students and faculty. I served on the telescope team, gave an opening lecture on the Sun in 2015, and in 2016, the year of the first gravitational wave detection, led the exhibition at the gravitational wave booth.
